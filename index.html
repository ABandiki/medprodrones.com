About MedPro

Bridging the Last Mile in Healthcare

In Zimbabwe, geography and infrastructure create barriers between patients and the medical supplies they need. Rural clinics wait days for blood samples to reach labs. Pharmacies struggle to restock critical medications on time.

MedPro Drones solves this with an end-to-end drone logistics platform purpose-built for healthcare. We connect every stakeholder in a single, real-time system.

How It Works

From Request to Delivery in Minutes

A streamlined process that removes bottlenecks from medical supply chains.

1

Request Submitted

A healthcare facility, lab, or pharmacy submits a supply request through the MedPro platform.

2

Drone Dispatched

The system identifies the optimal route and dispatches a drone with real-time tracking enabled.

3

Delivery Confirmed

The recipient confirms delivery, the system logs the transaction, and oversight bodies have full visibility.
